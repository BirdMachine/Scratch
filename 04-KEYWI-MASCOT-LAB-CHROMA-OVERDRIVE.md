### GLASS GRAMMAR

| LAYER | JOB | PERSONALITY |
|---|---|---|
| dark transmission field | depth | nearly black teal / violet |
| doubled boundary | thickness | front + displaced rear surface |
| sharp white reflection | curvature | narrow, asymmetric |
| cyan ghost | cool edge | optical / structural |
| RGB fringe | refraction | only at edges/interfaces |
| bloom | event confirmation | brief, localized, never soup |

<p align="center"><img src="furniture/58-meter-green.svg" width="29%"> <img src="furniture/58-meter-magenta.svg" width="29%"> <img src="furniture/58-meter-sinebow.svg" width="29%"></p>

**Important:** rainbow belongs at optical interfaces. The chassis can stay smoked/dark/metallic while its *edges* behave irresponsibly. This makes the chroma feel luminous instead of merely colorful.

### 🗃️ EXPERIMENT NAMING RULE

From this chamber onward, experiment Markdown files begin with a zero-padded generation number:

`01-...md` → `02-...md` → `03-...md` → **`04-...md`**

That way GitHub's ordinary filename sort is also the lab chronology. Existing older chambers can be renamed/backfilled separately without changing their contents.

### Asset note

Mascot PNGs are expected at `assets/keywi03/`. They remain raster stickers; SVG is reserved for the machine furniture and animation.
